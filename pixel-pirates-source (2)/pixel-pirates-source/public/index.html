Platform

Everything a brand-protection desk needs

Built like a security operations center (SOC) console: register what's real, check anything that looks suspicious, and follow every case through to takedown.

Official brand registry

Record each brand's real domain, social handle and app publisher, so there's a trusted baseline to compare against.

Digital risk scanner

Paste a suspicious profile, page, domain or app listing and get a scored verdict in under a second.

Explainable signals

Every point of risk comes with a plain-English reason, from edit distance to scam phrases, so you can defend each takedown.

Investigations

Turn high-risk scans into cases. Track them from open to takedown requested to resolved, with analyst notes.

Live SOC dashboard

Risk distribution, two-week scan trend, most-targeted brands and an activity feed, all read straight from the database.

Full audit trail

Workflow

How it works

Four steps from "that looks off" to a documented takedown.

  1. 01

    Register

    Add your official brand assets: domain, social handle and app publisher.

  2. 02

    Submit a candidate

    Enter the suspicious name, domain, handle, publisher or post text.

  3. 03

    Analyse & score

    The engine checks typosquats, homoglyphs, combosquats, publisher mismatches and scam language.

  4. 04

    Investigate

    Open a case from any scan, add notes, and track it through to takedown.
